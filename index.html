ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A school built on discipline, curiosity and care

Since 2011, Mwanza International Academy has offered a rounded education across primary and secondary levels, combining a rigorous curriculum with strong pastoral care and a wide range of co-curricular activities.

Mission

To nurture confident, capable learners who lead with integrity in their communities.

Vision

To be the region's most trusted school for academic and personal growth.

Principal's message
